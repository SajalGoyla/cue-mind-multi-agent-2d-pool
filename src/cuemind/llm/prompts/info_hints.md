## Physics hints in the candidate lines
After the geometry, each pot also carries a physics hint worked out from the geometry with standard pool rules (it is an estimate, not a simulation):
- where the cue ball goes after contact: roughly how far it travels, whether it bounces off cushions, and where it should stop;
- how easy your next shot should be from there, and on which ball;
- a WARNING when the cue ball's path runs towards a pocket (scratch risk).
Use the hints to choose between the speed and spin variants of the same pot: prefer the variant with no scratch warning and the best next shot.
