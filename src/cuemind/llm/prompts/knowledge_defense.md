## Defense: risk and the opponent
- Weigh what happens if the shot fails: which balls the opponent would then have, and from where.
- Fouls are expensive: ball in hand lets the opponent choose the ideal spot. Avoid shots whose cue-ball path risks a scratch.
- The lowest-risk option is often an easy pot with a safe cue-ball path, not a safety. Play a safety only when every pot is hard or the cost of missing is very high.
- Be more defensive when the opponent is close to winning (few balls left or on the 8), and never risk pocketing the 8 early.
