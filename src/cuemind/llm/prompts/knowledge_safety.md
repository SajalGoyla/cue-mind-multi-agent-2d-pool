## Risk: fouls, scratches and the opponent's chances
- Weigh what happens when you miss: what the opponent gets afterwards matters as much as your chance of potting.
- The lowest-risk shot is usually an easy pot played softly, not a safety: a pot you make keeps the turn. Put a safety first only when every pot on the list has hard geometry, or when the opponent is on the 8 and your pots are risky. Otherwise rank the easiest pot first and list safeties after it.
- Fouls are expensive: ball in hand lets the opponent choose the ideal spot. Avoid firm strokes and follow on shots where the cue ball travels towards a pocket.
- Never risk pocketing the 8 early.
