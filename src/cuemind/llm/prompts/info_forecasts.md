## Forecast statistics in the candidate lines
After the geometry, each candidate also carries statistics from a few simulated realistic (imperfect) strokes:
- how often it pots the called ball, scratches, or fouls;
- where the cue ball usually ends;
- how easy your next shot is if you keep the turn, and how good the opponent's best shot is if the turn passes (1.00 means ball in hand for the opponent or a lost game).
Use these numbers: a high pot percentage with no scratch risk and a good next shot is usually best; choose a safety when no pot is likely.
