## Offense: potting
- A missed pot usually hands the table to the opponent, so the shot must go in: prefer easy geometry (small cut, short distances) over hard geometry.
- Among variants of the same pot, avoid the spin and speed that risk sending the cue ball into a pocket; a scratch gives the opponent ball in hand.
- Count how many of your balls are realistically pottable from here. When you can run out, potting is worth more.
- When you are on the 8, only the 8 matters: choose the most reliable pot on it and avoid any scratch risk.
