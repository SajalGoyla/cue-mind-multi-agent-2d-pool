## Offense: potting
- The most important number is the probability of potting the called ball with a realistic stroke. A pot that goes in rarely usually hands the table to the opponent.
- Prefer shots with high pot probability and low scratch and foul risk; a scratch gives the opponent ball in hand.
- Count how many of your balls are realistically runnable from here. When you can run out, potting is worth more.
- When you are on the 8, only the 8 matters: be sure of the pocket and avoid any scratch risk.
