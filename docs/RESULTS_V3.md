# CueMind v3 results: equal-information conditions

Run 2026-11-26/27 on branch `v3-games`, prompts `prompts-v3`, Qwen 2.5 3B Instruct for every LLM agent, same 30 seeds as v2 (9000-9029), seats swapped, common random numbers, playing noise `none` and `low`. 600 games, 0 errors (condition A 117 min, condition B 235 min). Design: D-031 in `docs/decisions.md`. All numbers: `results/summary.md` (generated by `experiments/analyze.py`).

## Conditions

| | What every player sees | Reference player |
|---|---|---|
| v2 (earlier) | Stat: forecasts; SA/MA: geometry only | Stat |
| **v3-A: no forecasts for anyone** | geometry + simulation-free physics hints; menu chosen without simulation | Rule (fixed hint-based rule) |
| **v3-B: forecasts for everyone** | geometry + forecast statistics (3 noisy simulations per option) | Stat |

## Main results: win rate against the reference player (60 games each, 95% Wilson CI)

| Condition | Playing noise | SA | MA | Paired MA - SA (wins per seed, of 2) | sign test p |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| v2 (information gap) | none | 22% [13, 34] | 25% [16, 37] | +0.07 [-0.17, +0.30] | 0.77 |
| v2 (information gap) | low | 37% [26, 49] | 33% [23, 46] | -0.07 [-0.37, +0.23] | 1.0 |
| **v3-A (no forecasts)** | none | 38% [27, 51] | **50% [38, 62]** | **+0.23 [-0.13, +0.60]** | 0.48 |
| **v3-A (no forecasts)** | low | 45% [33, 57] | 45% [33, 57] | +0.00 [-0.23, +0.27] | 1.0 |
| **v3-B (forecasts for all)** | none | 37% [26, 49] | 33% [23, 46] | -0.07 [-0.37, +0.23] | 1.0 |
| **v3-B (forecasts for all)** | low | 48% [36, 61] | 53% [41, 65] | +0.10 [-0.20, +0.40] | 0.39 |

Controls: the breaker's win rate in self-play was 40-57%, with no seat advantage in any condition.

## Findings

1. **SA vs MA (the research question): no detectable difference in any of the six comparisons.** Every paired interval includes zero. The largest gap is v3-A with no noise (MA +0.23 wins per seed, MA better on 11 seeds, SA on 7, p = 0.48). It is suggestive, but within noise at 30 seeds.
2. **Equal information closes most of the gap to the reference player.** With forecasts for everyone (B), SA and MA play almost as well as Stat at low noise (48-53%) and improve on v2 with no noise (33-37% vs 22-25%). The v2 gap was mostly an information gap.
3. **Forecasts improve the agents' own play.** At the shot level (all decisions):

   | Shot-level, no noise | v2 | v3-A | v3-B |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | pot success SA / MA | 0.93 / 0.95 | 0.89 / 0.92 | 0.98 / 0.98 |
   | scratch per decision SA / MA | 0.052 / 0.047 | 0.058 / 0.072 | 0.030 / 0.035 |
   | same pick as Stat's objective SA / MA | 15% / 18% | - | 24% / 27% |

   Even with the numbers in front of them, the 3B agents choose Stat's top option only about a quarter of the time.
4. **The physics hints are a fair but rough substitute.** Against the hint-based Rule player, the agents reach 38-50%. The agents avoided about half of the warned shots: warned shots were 5-7% of the pots on the menu and 3-3.5% of the agents' choices, while Rule never chose one. When an agent did choose a warned shot it scratched 50-54% of the time, against 7-8% otherwise. The warnings are accurate, but they miss most scratches (recall 47%, `results/v3_hint_validation.md`).
5. **Cost:** MA uses 3.6-3.7 calls, about 3.4x the input tokens and about 4x the latency of SA in every condition. The specialists agreed (coordinator skipped) in 43-53% of v3 decisions, against 26-27% in v2: richer information makes the specialists agree more.

## Interpretation for the report

- With a 3B model, splitting the decision among offense, position and risk specialists plus a coordinator does not measurably beat one agent with the same knowledge, under three information regimes and two noise levels, at about 4x the cost.
- What the agents are told matters much more than how they are organized. Forecasts lift pot success to 98% and halve scratches; the agents' weakness in v2 was missing information, not missing teamwork.
- The one place the team might help is the least-informed setting that still has something to reason about (v3-A, no noise). Testing that needs more seeds or a larger model.

## Limitations

- **Power:** 60 games per pairing detect only gaps of about 0.25 wins per seed; smaller team effects are not excluded.
- **Not comparable across conditions:** win rates are against different opponents in A (Rule) and B (Stat); only the shot-level numbers compare across conditions.
- **Hint calibration:** the hint constants were fitted on dev-state simulations (rules of thumb; never main seeds); the hints remain approximate.
- **Model and prompts:** a single 3B model and a single prompt set (`prompts-v3`).
